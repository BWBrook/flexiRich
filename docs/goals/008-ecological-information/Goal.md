## Scientific contract

Keep the historical reference-period camera-accessible membership estimand
explicit. Distinguish it from species use during a particular window, and from
the number and identities discovered with a specified future deployment.
Past detections establish membership of a reference period containing those
dates under correct identification; they do not establish availability during
every later short window. No permanent availability layer, candidate-pool
change or ecological class reassignment is made in this tranche.

Same-grid repeated observations must preserve persistent species dependence.
Transferring a generic class distribution to independent species is a separate
experiment. New-reserve transfer additionally requires uncertain spatial
transport. A generating class mean, a realised finite-species mean and survey
miss probabilities must be reported as distinct quantities.
