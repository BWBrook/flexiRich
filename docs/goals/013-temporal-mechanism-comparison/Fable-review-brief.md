# Goal013: brief for Fable's scientific challenge

Prepared 9 October 2026 for Barry; not posted to the Message Board.

The 500-dataset oracle screen is complete and assessed. Please read
[Decision.md](Decision.md), the figure and compact tables in
`evidence/assessment-v1/`, and the independent results review. The frozen
contract remains [Goal.md](Goal.md), source `df8274015ab5deb9667ce81e2ac545f50f602c73`.

Primary own-truth selections are static-compatible 95/100, harmonic 99/100,
array 100/100 and station 100/100. Two numerical flags remain failures. The
mechanisms separate under known full-period placement margins, but this does
not identify the field mechanism. Array truth produces mammal cross-station
odds ratios centred at 3.615 versus 1.323 observed. The bird harmonic matches
the half-year/year pattern but misses strong adjacent-week clustering. Bird
state amplitudes hit the upper bound in 18% of own-array and 30% of own-station
first-year fits. Rare-species state-model forecast gains are very small.

Your service-drift concern is supported as a possible confound: shared service
stress selects station states in 86/100 first-year comparisons, then array and
station states in 26/100 each after two training years. The service realization
is fixed across replicates, and its prescribed strength does not explain all
observed clustering. Camera performance itself remains unidentified.

The provisional next step is to estimate training-only placement/service
nuisance structure, compare taxon-specific annual variation, and test combined
truths with nuisance and temporal-parameter uncertainty. We would retain state
models as residual candidates while keeping membership fixed initially.

Please challenge five points:

1. Does the field-diagnostic mismatch justify withholding advancement of the
   present array template despite perfect oracle recovery? Which observation
   would most strongly falsify that judgment?
2. What is the smallest service/seasonal comparison that can separate useful
   nuisance adjustment from an over-flexible fit, given nine stations and
   uneven service intervals? Specify the necessary combined truth and controls.
3. Should residual short-term clustering first be tested with local states,
   a shared component, or a simpler encounter-overdispersion process? Explain
   which paired diagnostic discriminates them; pooled odds ratios alone do not
   partition causal variation.
4. What forecasting gain for training-undetected known members would justify
   the added complexity once margins must be estimated? Aggregate recovery and
   conservative count coverage appear insufficient.
5. Is clarification of bird labeling sufficient to test a temporal trend, or
   is genuinely new observation effort needed before that interpretation is
   useful? The already-open Judbury record cannot become fresh validation.

Please preserve contrary evidence and propose a finite comparison with a
stopping criterion. No request to refit the completed run, revise the species
pool, open Huon Ridge, or interpret latent states as local absence is implied.
