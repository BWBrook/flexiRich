# Independent interpretation review

19 September 2026. A separate methodological specialist inspected the completed
Goal010 summaries, controls, sampler attempts, bridge outputs, prior checks and
the inherited forecast contract. Read-only; no later or raw detections accessed.
The parent independently verified numerical comparisons and file identities.

Verdict: the spatial mechanism comparison succeeds, while the temporal model
and unseen-tail inference remain inadequate for general richness claims. The
review supports freezing existing forecasts now, with primary shared-station
versus primary spatial as the main comparison and four prior sensitivities.
Adding seasonality, AR or latent availability first would require a new temporal
transport assumption that one development year does not identify.

Review findings retained in the decision:

- The spatial PPC improvements are in-sample model criticism, not calibrated
  coverage or out-of-sample validation. The selected crossing is exploratory.
- The prior family puts little mass on almost invisible members under the
  first-year effort. Similar richness after spatial repair cannot certify the
  unseen tail, which remains dominated numerically by undetected bird candidates.
- All-zero candidates are exactly exchangeable. Pool their named marginal
  forecast probabilities; preserve heterogeneous per-draw latent probabilities
  before integrating the discovery-count PMF. Label subgroup predictions as
  consequences of exchangeability, not taxon-specific biological information.
- Preserve a joint nested-horizon object. Four marginal count distributions
  are not four independent forecast replicates.
- Retain the camera/interaction decomposition limitation and fixed-array scope.
- Three rescue fits reflect the prespecified ESS/R-hat policy; final fits pass
  unchanged criteria, with no divergences or depth hits in any attempt.

A focused independent audit of the new forecast code and frozen objects is
required before later outcomes are opened. It will check joint posterior
pairing, effort/calendar endpoints, probability and score numerics, named
candidate symmetry, horizon dependence, primary/sensitivity roles and the
recorded historical exposure. No additional Goal010 fit is needed.
