## 4. What do the 'bl' and 's1' to 's5' columns in hr_service_data.xlsx record?

While looking for service dates, Claude printed the first six rows of this September 2025 service summary once. Options:

- [ ] counts: Image or trigger counts
- [ ] non_detection: Battery levels or other non-detection fields
- [x] mixed: Some of each; see comment

Selected: mixed

Comment:

bl = "battery life". It's actually the mV reading measured by the camera unit. For these disposable lithiums, anything above 6K is considered "healthy when serviced". Below that is an indication that one or more cells might be close to failure. In short, it's just a rough guide to me, as the field operator, to the camera's battery-drain status when serviced. It allows me to watch out for cameras that might be doing strange things, but is not relevant to our analysis here.

s1, s2 etc. are just columns indicating how many days the camera ran in the previous service. Again, it was more for me to see if cameras were failing early more than once. If the integers were bold, then it meant they were still operating when serviced. I subsequently switched to a 4-month service rotation, to minimise downtime. Again, more for me as the field operator than of relevance to our analysis, since we'll read the operational records (recorded or inferred) elsewhere.
